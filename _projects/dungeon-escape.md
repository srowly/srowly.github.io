*This project is currently in development. More to come.*

Dungeon Escape is a first-person 3D dungeon crawler built in Unreal Engine C++, featuring a series of interactive environmental puzzles across a dark, atmospheric dungeon setting.

The genre has personal roots. Games like **Might and Magic**, **Dungeon Master**, and **Eye of the Beholder** were formative — that feeling of moving through a dungeon where every room might contain something that kills you, a lever that opens something three corridors back, or a puzzle that actually requires thinking. Recreating a piece of that in Unreal is the goal.

Following Obstacle Assault, this is the project where the foundations move beyond tutorial scope: custom interaction systems, environmental storytelling, first-person mechanics, and puzzle design that actually works. Development has started. Updates will follow here.
